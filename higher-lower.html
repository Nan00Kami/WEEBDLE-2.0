About Anime Rank Duel (Higher or Lower)

Anidle Rank Duel tests your global anime popularity perception. Players are presented with pairs of notable anime from our master index. Your objective is to deduce whether the contender on the right holds a higher standing (closer to rank #1) or a lower standing on global MyAnimeList leaderboards than the benchmark anime on the left.

Rules & 10-Streak Victory Condition

  • Ranking Polarity: Remember that MyAnimeList rank #1 (such as Frieren) represents the highest possible score, while rank #500 represents a comparatively lower standing.
  • Winning the Gauntlet: Guess correctly across 10 successive rounds to beat the duel. An incorrect answer immediately halts the run and logs your final score.
  • Data Synchronization: Metadata and rankings are sourced from our verified Anime Directory.

Overview

At Anidle (accessible via anidle.com), the privacy of our visitors is important to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by Anidle and how we use it.

Cookies and Local Storage

Anidle uses standard browser localStorage to store client-side gameplay information, such as your completed guesses, daily streaks, and cached catalog entries. This data remains on your local device and is not collected or transmitted to external servers.

Third-Party Vendors & Advertising

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve advertisements based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to Anidle and/or other sites on the internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Log Files

Like many web services, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may automatically log visitor requests, including internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser types, internet service providers (ISPs), date/time stamps, and referring/exit pages. These logs are used solely for analyzing trends, administering the site, and monitoring system security.

    About Character Anidle

    Character Anidle tests your character recall across legendary anime series. Each day at 00:00 local time, a mystery character is picked from our roster. Deduce their identity by matching their parent series, canonical genre roots, biological gender, and the ultimate playground debate parameter: Can they beat Son Goku?

    The Goku Metric Explained

    The "Beats Goku?" indicator evaluates universal reality warping, conceptual immortality, and dimensional hacks. If a character possesses causality manipulation (like Giorno Giovanna or Haruhi Suzumiya) or infinite erasure hax, the field flags YES. Normal human combatants, sports athletes, and standard shonen brawlers flag NO.

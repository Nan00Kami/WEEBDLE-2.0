    About Character Anidle

    Character Anidle tests your anime memory across iconic series. Each day at 00:00 local time, a mystery character is chosen from our catalog. Deduce their identity using clues based on their parent anime, air year, canonical genres, biological gender, and whether they can defeat Son Goku.

    Understanding the Clues

    • Air Year: Shows whether the character's parent series aired earlier (↓) or later (↑) than your guess.
    • Beats Goku?: Flags YES for conceptual reality-warping entities, multiversal deities, and reality-erasers; NO for normal human fighters, martial artists, and standard brawlers.

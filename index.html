    Frequently Asked Questions

    When does the puzzle refresh?

    A new mystery anime is selected automatically each day at 00:00 local time.

    Where do the rankings and statistics come from?

    Metadata, release periods, canonical genres, and numerical rankings are referenced from indexed community databases including MyAnimeList and AniList.

    Can I play previous days?

    Currently, Anidle features one shared daily puzzle per cycle. Your streaks and game history are saved to your local browser storage.

    HOW TO PLAY

    Guess the secret anime in 6 tries. Each guess reveals hints based on official MyAnimeList data.

    🟩 Green: Exact match for that column.

    🟨 Yellow (Genres): Shares at least one genre with the target.

    ⬆️ Up Arrow: The secret anime's value is higher (or ranked higher on MAL).

    ⬇️ Down Arrow: The secret anime's value is lower.

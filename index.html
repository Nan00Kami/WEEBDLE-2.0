    About Anidle

    Anidle is a free, daily browser-based deduction game inspired by popular daily word puzzles, custom-built for the anime community. Every midnight, a new mystery anime from an extensive catalog spanning multiple decades and genres is selected as the daily answer. Players have six attempts to discover the target title using comparative cues.

    How Clues and Feedback Work

    • Release Year: Tells you if the mystery show was released earlier (↓) or later (↑) than your guess.
    • MyAnimeList Rank: Compares the all-time ranking on MAL. An upward arrow (↑) indicates that the mystery title has a better (lower numerical) position.
    • Genres: Displays highlighted tags when your guess shares canonical genres with the mystery target.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    When does the puzzle reset? The puzzle updates automatically at midnight local time every 24 hours.

    Where are statistics sourced from? Show data, popularity ranks, and canonical metadata reflect verified listings from open anime archives.

About Anidle

What is Anidle?

Anidle is a daily browser deduction game created for anime fans. Inspired by traditional word puzzle formats, the game challenges players to determine a single mystery anime title each day through iterative, comparative feedback.

How It Works

Every 24 hours at midnight local time, an anime is algorithmically selected from a curated pool of notable releases. Players receive up to six guesses to identify the target title. With every attempt, comparative markers provide clues:

  • Release Year: Shows whether the mystery title aired earlier or later than the guessed title.
  • MyAnimeList Rank: Shows whether the target is ranked higher or lower numerically in global popularity and rating lists.
  • Genres: Highlights shared canonical genres between the guessed anime and the secret answer.

Anidle Daily Puzzle Log & Archive

Welcome to the historical puzzle archive of Anidle. Each day at 00:00 local time, our deterministic scheduling engine picks a secret anime from our master catalog. This archive chronicles recent daily mystery challenges, giving players an opportunity to review past solutions, compare clue parameters, and study historical trends.

Puzzle Rotation Mechanics

The daily puzzle cycle calculates its index through an elapsed-day offset anchored to January 1, 2024. Each mystery anime challenges deduction across three balanced axes:

  • Chronological Proximity: Did the title air earlier or later than your guess?
  • Reputational Tier: Is the title ranked higher or lower in global community standing?
  • Thematic Convergence: Which canonical genres overlap between the target and your attempts?
